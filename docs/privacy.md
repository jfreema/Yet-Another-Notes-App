# YANA privacy policy

*Last updated November 21, 2026*

YANA (Yet Another Notes App), for iPhone and Mac, doesn't collect any data about you.

- **Your lists and notes stay on your devices.** YANA keeps them on your iPhone or Mac. It has no servers and no
  account, and it doesn't send anything to its developer or to anyone else.
- **Syncing goes through a folder you choose.** If you sync a list, YANA writes it to the folder you picked, such as
  one in iCloud Drive, and reads your other devices' copies from there. Moving those files between your devices is
  done by that folder's service (for example iCloud Drive, under Apple's privacy policy), not by YANA. Each device in
  the folder has a small file with the name you gave it, so your other devices can show it.
- **No tracking, analytics or ads.** YANA has no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.
- **Removing your data.** Deleting YANA removes what it keeps on that device. To remove synced copies, delete the
  YANA folders (lists and devices) in your sync folder.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/jfreema/Yet-Another-Notes-App/issues.
